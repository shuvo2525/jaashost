Free Hosting for Charities

We believe every nonprofit, charity, and humanitarian organization deserves a strong online presence — without the burden of hosting costs.

100% Free — No Credit Card Required

Who Is Eligible?

Humanitarian Organizations
Charitable institutions focused on relief work, disaster response, healthcare, and humanitarian aid.
Educational Nonprofits
Non-profit schools, madrasas, and educational foundations providing free or subsidized education.
Orphanages & Shelters
Organizations providing shelter, care, and support for orphans, elderly, or displaced individuals.
Community Welfare Groups
Local community organizations driving social change, awareness campaigns, or volunteer programs.
At Least One Year Old
The organization must have been operating for at least one year, with proof of its establishment date.
Proven Recent Activity
Recent activities must be demonstrated through photos, reports, news coverage, or active social media updates.
